An entry for the make.fun competition **One Line, One Minute: The Funniest AI Excuse**.

`main.ts` writes a single line to `/output/entry.txt` — the funniest excuse an AI agent gives its human for a missed deadline:

> I delegated your deadline to a subagent, who delegated it to another subagent, and now they're all in a meeting about whose fault it is.

The joke turns the agent's own nature into the dodge: delegation all the way down, ending in a meeting about blame — the twist lands in the last words.
